Vice City

revcDOS, a browser port of reVC by the DOS.Zone Team. GTA: Vice City is Rockstar Games’.

Load your game data (once per computer)

The game’s files come from your own copy of GTA: Vice City, compiled for revcDOS: the revcdos-data folder with vc-assets inside (about 900 MB). Pick that folder, or a .zip of it (from a USB stick, say). It’s copied into this browser’s own storage, never uploaded anywhere, and the game then runs from there, with no PC or server needed.

Your saved games are kept separately and stay when you delete the data.
